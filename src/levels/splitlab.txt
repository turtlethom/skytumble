title: Split Lab (prototypes)

=== splitlab-01
par: 6
name: Two of a Kind
idea: Stand on the splitter and the block splits in two: the second cube appears where its arrow points. Tab swaps which cube moves, and never counts as a move. Roll one cube into the other to join them, and join them in line with the corridor to climb to the goal.

=== splitlab-05
par: 7
name: Slipstream
idea: Glass tubes take only cubes, so split to ride one. A belt pushes only the cube you just moved: the first cube waits on a belt that points at the edge, and the second, pushed toward it, is held where it lands. Join them and drop onto the goal.
