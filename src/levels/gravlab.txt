title: Gravity Lab (prototypes)

=== gravlab-01
par: 11
name: Up and Over
idea: Violet rings flip gravity: stand on one and you fall up to the floor right above you. The soft shadow on your tiles is the other floor overhead. Hold Space to look at it: violet corners mark where each ring lands, and one ring here has nothing above it.

=== gravlab-02
par: 10
name: Open Sky
idea: Three rings, but a flip only works if there's a floor directly above. Read the shadow on your tiles, or hold Space to look: one ring drops you into the sky. Up there, mind the edges too: rolling off the ceiling is a fall.

=== gravlab-03
par: 11
name: Lie Down to Rise
idea: Two rings side by side, under fragile planks. Flip standing and you land standing on a plank, which breaks. Lie across both rings and you arrive lying down, safely.
