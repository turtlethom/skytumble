title: Shape Lab (prototypes)

=== shapelab-01
par: 11
name: Shape Demo
idea: Shape switches change you when you stand on them; the bar on each shows the shape: three long makes you tall, a small nub makes you a cube. Tall, tip across the gap and stand on the extra-heavy switch to raise the bridge. As a cube you're light enough to cross the planks, and this goal wants a cube.
